The Long Climb in JGB Yields Is Nearly Over

20 July 2026 · read the article

JGB yields saw a historic rise between 2022 and 2026. Most of the rise came from higher inflation expectations and a normalization of term premia. Our analysis shows the Long Climb is likely nearly over.
