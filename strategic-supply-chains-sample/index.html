Strategic supply chains: a free sample

20 November 2026

This is a free sample page from the Strategic Supply Chain Monitor, a new feature of the Yen Macro Monitors pages, available for paid subscribers. The Strategic Supply Chain Monitor includes graphical analysis of supply chains in key industries: how the hub for each industry changed, in the world and in Asia, between 2000 and 2025. As of November 20, 2026, preliminary representations are available for Transportation equipment, Semiconductors, Pharmaceuticals, Rare Earths and the overall intermediate goods.

Sample: motor-vehicle parts trade in Asia

Chart 1

Motor-vehicle parts trade among 12 Asian economies, 2000-2025

Opens with a short guide to reading the chart (about 1 min 50 s). One scale for all years.

One finding from each page

Asia and transport

  • Intermediate goods: China was the largest supplier of intermediate goods to 2 of the other 11 Asian economies in 2000 and to 10 in 2025.
  • Transportation equipment: Japan supplied 74% of the motor-vehicle parts traded among 12 Asian economies in 2000 and 26% in 2025; China supplied 3.5% and 37%.

Other industries

  • Semiconductors: Taiwan was the largest direct supplier of chips to the US, the EU, Japan, Korea, the UK, Canada, Australia and China in 2025.
  • Pharmaceuticals: by weight, China supplied 73% of US medicine-ingredient imports in 2025, 76% of the EU's and 83% of Japan's.
  • Rare Earths: by weight, China supplied 72% of Japan's imports of metal permanent magnets in 2008 and 33% in 2025.

Each name opens its page. The full pages add interactive charts, every importer and supplier, the notes on the data, and annual updates.
