What is actually in a 10-year JGB yield

10Y spot yield decomposed into expected policy rates and the term premium

Source: JMA JGB yield-curve model, vintage 19 July 2026. Lightened bars and the dotted line from August 2026 = JMA baseline projection.

The two blocks sum to the black line at every point. Interest rate expectations are the average short-term rate the market expects over the bond's life; the term premium is the extra it demands for bearing duration and other risks. A negative term premium hangs below the zero line, so the yield sits below the top of the blue block.

The policy rate and what the market expects of it

BoJ policy rate against the risk-neutral component at 2Y and 10Y

Source: JMA JGB yield-curve model, vintage 19 July 2026. Policy rate is actual in history and an assumed path in the projection.

The gap between the policy rate and the 2-year risk-neutral rate is the near-term tightening the market has already priced. The 10-year version is where the market thinks rates settle.
