JGB yield curve forecast — Alternative Forecast: Faster and Further

Forecast vintage: August 2026, model origin July 2026. Estimates are revised periodically, and updated estimates are available for paid users upon request.

The JMA yield curve model splits each JGB yield into interest-rate expectations and a term premium, letting Japan’s long-run interest rate move with its fundamentals. This page carries the forecast under our Alternative scenario, in which the Bank of Japan moves sooner and goes further than our central case assumes. The model itself — what it is, what goes into it, and the historical decomposition it produces — is set out on the JGB yield curve model page. The charts below are interactive. Hover for exact values, zoom, and download the data behind each chart.

What this forecast assumes

Bank of Japan policy. The policy rate is 1.00 per cent at the forecast origin. It rises by 25 basis points in September 2026, again in March 2027 and again in December 2027, reaching a terminal rate of 1.75 per cent. That is one increase more than our Main Forecast assumes, and the first of them comes a month earlier.

The US term premium. The forecast carries an assumed rise in the US 10-year term premium — the extra compensation demanded for holding long-dated Treasuries — following the change of Federal Reserve chair at the end of May 2026. It builds from 67 basis points at end-May 2026 to a peak of 122 basis points at end-November 2026, then unwinds part-way, with half the rise retained at 95 basis points by the end of the horizon. In our model, about a quarter of a US term-premium move passes through into superlong JGB yields. This assumption is identical to the one in the Main Forecast.

The shaded band. The band around each forecast path is a one-standard-deviation range built from the historical distribution of twelve-month moves in the US 10-year term premium. By 2029 it spans roughly plus or minus 17 basis points at 10 years and 20 basis points at 30 years. It is an uncertainty range around the US term-premium assumption alone, not a confidence interval around the whole forecast.

Origin and vintage. The forecast runs monthly from July 2026 to July 2029. It is cut from a July 2026 month-end origin, which is a later starting point than the 19 July 2026 vintage published on the model page, so the starting yields on the two pages differ slightly.
