BoJ-Policy Stance Indicator

Free edition: the record through the policy board meeting of 28 April 2026, frozen at that date and not refreshed. BoJ-PSI is an ongoing project, and scores are revised when later evidence arrives; the revisions, and every meeting since, are in the paid edition.

BoJ-PSI is a text-based analysis of the Bank of Japan's communications. The Bank publishes a Summary of Opinions six business days after its policy board meeting, followed by more detailed minutes one to two months later. Remarks are anonymous, but we devised a method to make attribution to members with a varying level of confidence.

Each member is scored on three dimensions — Economy, Inflation and Policy Rate — on a 0–10 scale where 5 is neutral. A score around 7 on the Policy Rate dimension indicates advocacy of a standard rate hike by 0.25%. Scoring members separately on Economy and Inflation enables us to see not just whether a member is a hawk or dove, but why.

Every attribution is graded on a confidence tier, and only sufficiently confident attributions feed PSI scores. We treat each one as an assessed inference, not an established fact. This free edition stops at the April 2026 meeting. The paid edition adds updated member scores after each Summary of Opinions and each set of minutes.
